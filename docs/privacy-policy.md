# Debt Destroyer privacy policy and terms

The privacy policy and terms of use are published on countersunk.dev:

- Privacy policy: https://countersunk.dev/apps/debt-destroyer/privacy-policy/
- Terms of use: https://countersunk.dev/apps/debt-destroyer/tos/

Their source is in the `countersunkweb` repository, under `apps/debt-destroyer/`. The app links to both from setup and from Settings → Privacy (`lib/core/links.dart`).

If what the app collects changes (for example, a new diagnostic event in `lib/app/diagnostic_events.dart`), update the privacy policy there, and update the store disclosures in `docs/release.md`.
